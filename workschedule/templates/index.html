Privacy First

Zero Retention, Zero Tracking

🛡️ Stateless Processing

This platform is designed as a stateless SaaS. Photos and files pass temporarily into secure, isolated cloud memory buckets solely to perform parsing computations, and are immediately vaporized. We do not keep logs, images, user profiles, or historic schedules.

💳 Secure Payments

Financial infrastructure is entirely handled securely via Stripe. Because we hold no data on the site, your personal information is never aggregated, profile-built, or sold to third-party marketing entities.
